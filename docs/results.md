## Limits — state these, don't hide them

- Fitted per country, which stands in for region; no land-cover stratification yet.
- Matching is spatial-temporal proximity (same UTC date, ~3.5 km), not pixel-footprint overlap.
- Cell-month scores are noisy at ~250 km² resolution; small-fire countries (Portugal) score lowest.
- The harmonized record from 2012 is derived from Suomi-NPP, which did not drift, so it is not exposed to Aqua's orbital drift. The drift-era table shows what a naive Aqua-based record would get wrong.
- Claims are regional; no global step correction is claimed.

## What the held-out scores are computed on (evaluation population)

Every held-out score above (R², bias, RMSE; MAE in `validation_points.json`) uses one table per
country: H3 res-5 cell × month, 2019–2021, built by the full outer join in
`scripts/fit_harmonization.py` (`fit_country.evaluate`). A cell-month is included when Aqua **or**
Suomi-NPP recorded at least one filtered daytime vegetation-fire detection there; cell-months
where neither did are not in the table. Included zeros are kept: in the published sample
(`validation_points.json`, 48,645 cell-months, at most 300 per country) 63.7% have observed
Aqua = 0 and 5.4% have Aqua > 0 with no Suomi-NPP detection (sample shares, each country weighted
equally; not population totals). Across the 171 fitted countries the table has 2,573,665
held-out cell-months in all (sum of `n_cell_months`). Scores are on untransformed counts; R² is
relative to the mean of this table. Adding the both-zero cell-months would change every score, so
the numbers describe agreement where at least one record saw fire. Full definition:
`docs/science.md` §2a.

## Pooled ("borrowed") curves — how they are formed and how they do out of country

The pooled curve is a weighted mean of the fitted b0, b1 and κ of the 61 own-fit countries
fitted before it was formed, weight = training Suomi-NPP detections; recomputed from
`params_global.json` it matches the published values to floating-point precision. It is not a
logistic model fitted to pooled detections. Details: `docs/science.md` §2a.

No pooled country is one of the 61 sources, so their held-out scores test the curve out of
country (from `params_global.json`, nothing refitted):

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Own curve | 130 | 0.64 | 11 | 5.8% | 9 | 62 |
| Pooled curve, own κ | 10 | 0.35 | 3 | 13.4% | 3 | 7 |
| Pooled curve, pooled κ | 31 | −0.11 | 21 | 43.1% | 9 | 15 |

What these 21 have in common, compared with the other 150 fitted countries:

- **Mostly "less bad", not "good".** The cut-off R² is above 0.1 only in the United States (0.69)
  and Cyprus (0.50). In 19 of the 21 the model's held-out R² is negative. A high cut-off that
  predicts almost no detections (cut-off bias −4% to −100%, median −79%; below −45% in 19 of 21) scores near R² = 0 by
  predicting close to zero everywhere; the model scores worse because it over-predicts.
- **The model over-predicts.** Model held-out bias is positive in 20 of 21 (median |bias| 39%),
  against 95 positive of 150 elsewhere (median |bias| 7%); 14 of the 21 exceed 20%.
- **Small or borrowed samples.** 12 of the 21 use the pooled curve (12 of the 41 pooled fits); median
  held-out size 702 cell-months versus 3,471 for the rest.
- **Low co-detection rate.** Median training match rate (share of Suomi-NPP detections with an Aqua
  co-detection) 0.17, against 0.27 for the rest.
- **Not a test-year artefact.** In the **training** years the cut-off already has higher R² than the
  model in 15 of the 21 (5 of the other 150), so the weakness is visible without looking at the
  held-out years.
- **The ratio baseline also beats the model** in 18 of the 21.
- **Regions (by country, descriptive):** arid Middle East and North Africa (Iran, Saudi Arabia,
  Yemen, United Arab Emirates, Morocco); temperate, largely agricultural or industrial countries in
  North America, Europe and East Asia (United States, China, Spain, Germany, Japan, United Kingdom,
  Republic of Korea, Taiwan, Netherlands); small islands (Trinidad and Tobago, Puerto Rico, Solomon
  Islands, Cyprus, Brunei Darussalam, Comoros, Barbados). None is a large tropical savanna or forest
  fire country.

Not available from published files: the FRP distribution of each country's detections (only the
fitted 50%-detection FRP and the cut-off are published). Testing a different model for these
countries would need a validation split separate from 2019–2021; it has not been done, and the
published results stand as they are.
